Defense board

Drag anyone anywhere: their players (red, z1–z6 by rotation spot) and your hitter (blue). Tap a player to swap them between front row (dark) and back row. Write a note, save the situation, and export the whole log when you're done.

Meters: x across from your left sideline (0–9), y from the net (0) to their baseline (9). z1 is their right back, so it starts on your left.
